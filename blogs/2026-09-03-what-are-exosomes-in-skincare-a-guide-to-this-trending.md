Exosomes are tiny messengers released by cells that carry proteins, lipids, and genetic material between cells. In skincare, plant-derived exosomes are used in products designed to support skin that looks tired, uneven, or stressed. Rael uses Cica Exosome, derived from Centella Asiatica, in its Blemish Recovery Mask, a two-step system designed to support skin during the recovery stage after an active breakout.
