Menstruation on its own can often feel like a challenge if it's accompanied by symptoms such as cramps, bloating, breakouts, headaches, tender boobs, and mood swings. During summertime, you add in heat and humidity, and, well, it makes the experience far less comfortable. It's not the heat to blame, per se, but rather how it affects your stress levels.
