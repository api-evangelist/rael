Glutathione is commonly referred to in scientific literature as the body's "master antioxidant", a nickname that describes its role inside the body rather than what it does in a topical formula. It has recently made the jump from wellness supplements into skincare, especially in K-beauty. If you've seen it on an ingredient list and wondered what it's actually doing once it's on your skin, here's how it works and what it can realistically do for your complexion.
