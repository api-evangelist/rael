If your skin has ever gone red, tight, or itchy after a mask that was supposed to be relaxing, that reaction usually isn't random. Sensitive skin can react to fragrance, certain actives, or even a formula's texture, which can end up making Self-Care Sunday feel more like a gamble than a treat. The good news is that a mask doesn't have to be a risk.
