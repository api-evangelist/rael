Dealing with your period every month can be a bit of a challenge sometimes thanks to cramps, bloating, and other menstrual-related symptoms. In the summertime, however, it can feel even more challenging due to the heat, humidity, and the FOMO (fear of missing out) you feel when you’re invited to all sorts of fun summer activities. The good news is you don’t have to sit out on all the summer amusement just because it’s that time of the month for you.
